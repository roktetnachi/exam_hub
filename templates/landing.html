How it works

From a blank paper to released grades

Everything a teacher does with an exam happens in one place, in the order it happens in real life.

  1. 01

    Build

    Upload the texts and add questions in the order pupils will meet them.

    • PDF texts pupils can highlight and annotate
    • Long answers, multiple choice, tables, fill in the blank
    • Audio and video, with a limit on plays
  2. 02

    Deliver

    Open it now, at a set time or when you say — on laptops, Chromebooks or iPads.

    • Any browser, monitored, Safe Exam Browser or paper
    • Extra time for individual pupils
    • Spell check, calculators and read aloud set per exam
  3. 03

    Monitor

    See the whole room at a glance while the exam is running.

    • Who has started, handed in or gone offline
    • Who left the exam, and the code to let them back in
    • Messages to one pupil or the whole room
  4. 04

    Mark

    Comment on the words pupils wrote and on their PDF, then release grades when you're ready.

    • Comments pinned to the exact sentence
    • Objective questions mark themselves
    • Pupils see feedback only once you release it
  5. 05

    Export

    Keep a copy of every paper for moderation and records.

    • Each pupil's work as a PDF, with your comments
    • The whole class in one file or a zip
    • With or without marks

Hosting more than one school

Want Andam at your school?

Each school is set up separately and run by its own admins.

  • Your school's own web address, logo and colours on every page.
  • Year groups, subjects, teachers and pupils added from a spreadsheet.
  • Accounts sent by email. Sign in with a password or a one-time emailed code.
  • School admins see every exam and the activity log. Teachers see their own.
