Get your school andam — ready.

For schools thinking about Andam

From first message to first exam

Andam is licensed per school, per year. Each school gets its own private space: your pupils, staff and exams are never visible to another school.

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us your school's name, roughly how many pupils will use Andam and when you'd like to start. Use the Help button on this page{% if contact_email %} or email {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}.

  2. We set up your school

    We create your school's space with your name, logo, colour and time zone, and the main admin account. The main admin gets an email with a username and a temporary password.

  3. Your admin adds everyone

    Staff and pupils are added one by one or in bulk from an Excel template, then grouped into year groups and subjects. Andam emails each person their sign-in. The admin guide walks through it.

  4. Run a practice exam

    Before a real exam, give pupils a short practice paper so they meet the tools (highlighter, answer box, calculator, submit) when nothing is at stake.

Many question types

PDF papers pupils highlight and write about, long answers, multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank, tables, images, video and listening (audio).

Fair and secure

Leaving a secure exam locks it until the teacher's resume code is typed. Lockdown exams run in Safe Exam Browser.

Marking like pen on paper

Teachers select words to comment, drop pins on PDF pages, score each item and release grades when ready.

Accessibility built in

Pupils can change text size, font (easy-read and dyslexia), spacing and colours, and have text read aloud.

Extra time per pupil

Give an individual pupil extra minutes or early access without changing the exam for everyone else.

Works on laptops and iPads

Pupils use a normal browser. Work saves every few seconds and is kept on the device if the internet drops.

Guides by role

Your first steps

Each guide starts with the steps to do first, then what else you can do. You can replay the in-app tour any time from your account menu.

Your first hour as an admin

Work through these in order. Pupils can only be put into year groups and subjects once their accounts exist.

  1. Sign in and choose your password

    Your invite email has your username and a temporary password. The first time you sign in, Andam asks you to choose your own.

    Sign in page

  2. Add your staff

    Add one person at a time, or use Bulk add teachers: download the Excel template, fill in first name, last name, email, role (Teacher or Admin) and subjects, then upload it. You see a preview before anything is saved.

    Roles → Staff

  3. Add your pupils

    Add one pupil, or use Bulk add pupils with the template. The year group and subject columns are optional; you can fill those in later.

    Roles → Pupils

  4. Make year groups

    Create each year group, choose its teachers and enrol its pupils, one by one or with Bulk add pupils to year groups.

    Year groups

  5. Make subjects

    Create subjects, pick their teachers and add pupils from any year group (tick a whole year group to add everyone in it). Teachers can only set exams for pupils in the subjects they teach, so this step matters.

    Subjects

  6. Send the invites

    Each person shows as Not invited, Invited or Active. Send invites to one person, a selection or everyone not yet invited. If email isn't getting through, download the logins as a CSV and hand them out.

    Roles → Staff / Pupils

  7. Check it as a pupil

    Use Pupil preview to see exactly what pupils will see, read-only. It's a quick way to check an exam before it opens.

    Exams → Pupil preview

What else admins can do

Edit or remove people

Change a name or email from Roles → Edit. Delete pupils or staff one at a time or in bulk (bulk deletes ask you to type DELETE). Submitted work stays on the exams.

Pre-filled templates

Choose Template with current staff / pupils to download everyone already in the school, edit it and upload it again. Rows you don't change stay the same.

Activity log

A timeline of exams created, submissions, grading and year group or subject changes. Filter it by year group or type.

Every exam in the school

Admins can open, monitor and grade any teacher's exam, for cover lessons or moderation.

Safe Exam Browser

For Lockdown exams, school devices need Safe Exam Browser installed. Each Lockdown exam makes its own launch file; pupils just press Open in Safe Exam Browser.

Forgotten passwords

No reset needed from you: anyone can choose Email me a code on the sign-in page, sign in with the code and set a new password.

Tip: every person needs an email address. Pupils are matched to exams by email, and invites and sign-in codes go there.

Your first exam, step by step

  1. Check your subjects

    These are the pupils you can set exams for. If a subject or a pupil is missing, ask your school admin to add it.

    My subjects

  2. Press + New exam and add content

    Add items: a PDF paper pupils can highlight and write about, long answer, multiple choice (one or several answers), true/false, fill in the blank, table, image, video or audio. Give each item its points, group items into sections, and drag to reorder. Add Resources for pupils such as a formula sheet, map or source (PDF, image or text).

    Exams → + New exam → Content

  3. Choose how it's delivered

    Set when pupils can see it (visible now, at a set time, or hidden until you open it), when it ends (automatically at the end time or when you close it) and the delivery mode (see below). Then pick the tools pupils get: scientific calculator, graphing calculator, spell check, read aloud and a join code.

    + New exam → Delivery

  4. Assign it

    Tick a whole subject, or open it and pick individual pupils. Search finds a pupil quickly. Only you and your school's admins can see your exams.

    + New exam → Assign to

  5. Watch it live

    The Monitor tab shows who has started, handed in or been locked out. Message one pupil or all of them, give extra time or early access, unlock a pupil, or close the exam early.

    Open the exam → Monitor

  6. Mark and release

    In Grade, select words in an answer to comment, drop comment pins on PDF pages, and give each item a score and feedback. Next ungraded → (or N) moves you on. Pupils see nothing until you press Release grade for one pupil or the whole exam.

    Open the exam → Grade

Handy while it's running

Messages

Pupils can press Message teacher during an exam. You get a small pop-up and an item in the bell. Use Message all pupils for corrections.

Extra time

On Monitor, press Extra time on a pupil's row to give them extra minutes or early access, on top of the exam's own times.

Lockouts

A locked-out pupil pops up for you with their resume code. Read it out, or press Unlock now.

Change things mid-exam

From Settings you can add resources (pupils get them straight away), change the end time or who it's assigned to.

Automatic hand-in

When an exam closes, every pupil still working hands in automatically from their last save.

Export to PDF

From Grade, export one pupil or the whole class as PDFs, with or without scores, feedback and the exam's pages.

Tip: before your first real exam, set a five-minute practice exam in Any browser mode so pupils try highlighting, typing, the calculator and Submit.

Sitting your first exam

  1. Sign in

    Your school emails you a username and a temporary password. The first time you sign in you choose your own password. Forgot it? Choose Email me a code on the sign-in page.

    Sign in page

  2. Find your exam

    Your tasks are in three lists: To do, Submitted and Graded. The soonest deadline is at the top; one ending within a day shows in amber.

    My tasks

  3. Read the instructions

    Opening an exam shows its instructions: how many questions, the points, the end time and which tools you can use. The clock only starts when you press Start exam.

  4. Answer

    Highlight text on a PDF in different colours and add notes. Write in the answer box (bold, lists, tables, word count). Use the Notepad, Resources and the calculator if your teacher allowed them. Move between questions with ‹ › or open the question grid. Press ⚑ Flag to mark a question to come back to.

  5. Don't worry about saving

    Your work saves by itself every few seconds. If the internet drops, keep working: your answers are kept on your device and saved again when you're back online. Don't close the page while it says you're offline.

  6. Submit

    Press Submit. Andam lists any questions left blank or flagged so you can jump back to them. If the time runs out, your work is handed in for you.

  7. See your grade

    When your teacher releases it, the bell shows it and the exam gets a New grade label. Open it to see your score, feedback and your teacher's comments on your work.

Exam rules to know

Secure exams lock if you leave them. Switching tab or app, opening a new window or leaving fullscreen pauses the exam. Your teacher will read out a resume code; your work is safe.
Lockdown exams open in Safe Exam Browser. Press Open in Safe Exam Browser on the exam. It stays open until you submit, and closes by itself afterwards.
Stuck during an exam? Press Message teacher. The Help chat is turned off while you're in an exam.

Make it easier to read

The Accessibility menu in the top bar is always there, even during an exam.

Text size and font

Make text up to 175% bigger, and choose Standard, Easy-read or Dyslexia-friendly fonts.

Spacing and colours

Wider line and letter spacing, Night mode, high contrast or a coloured overlay.

Read aloud

Select words and press Read aloud, or press it with nothing selected to hear the whole question (if your teacher allows it).

Checklist

Before exam day

  • Every pupil shows  Active  on Roles (they've signed in and chosen a password).
  • Pupils have tried a practice exam with the same tools.
  • The exam is assigned to the right subject or pupils, and opens and closes at the right times.
  • Extra time and early access are set for pupils who need them.
  • For Lockdown exams, Safe Exam Browser is installed on every device.
  • Devices are charged and connected to the school Wi-Fi.
  • You've opened it in  Pupil preview  (admins) or checked it in the builder.
  • The Monitor tab is open on your screen when the exam starts.

Common questions

FAQ

I forgot my password.Everyone

On the sign-in page choose Email me a code, type your username or email, and enter the 6-digit code we email you. Then open your account menu → Change password; you won't need the old one for 15 minutes.

I didn't get my invite email.Everyone

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from Andam. If it isn't there, ask your school admin to press Resend on Roles, or to give you your login from the downloaded logins list.

A pupil is missing from my "Assign to" list.Teachers

Teachers can only assign to pupils in the subjects they teach. Ask an admin to add the pupil to your subject (Subjects page), then open the exam's Settings to tick them.

A pupil got locked out mid-exam.Teachers

Their resume code shows in your pop-up and on the Monitor tab. Read it out, or press Unlock now. Their work so far is saved.

The internet dropped during an exam.Pupils

Keep working. Andam keeps your answers on the device and saves them again by itself when you're back online. Don't close the page or sign out until it says your work is saved.

I can't see my grade.Pupils

Grades appear only after your teacher releases them. When they do, you'll see it in the bell and a New grade label on the exam.

Which devices work?Schools

Laptops, Chromebooks and iPads with an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). Lockdown exams need Safe Exam Browser, which runs on Windows, macOS and iPad.

Can other schools see our data?Schools

No. Each school has its own private space. Accounts only work in their own school, and exam files are only sent to people signed in to that school.

How do I get help?Everyone

Press the Help button at the bottom right of any page (except during an exam). It answers common questions, and Message support sends your question to our team by email. We reply to the email address you give.

Still stuck?

Ask the Help chat, or send a message straight to the Andam support team.
